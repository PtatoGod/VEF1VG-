What is Persona?

Persona … is a Japanese video game series and media franchise developed by Atlus and owned by Sega.

Persona's core features include a group of students as the main cast, a silent protagonist similar to the mainline Megami Tensei franchise, and combat using Personas. … Its overall theme is the exploration of the human psyche and how the characters find their true selves.

Source: Wikipedia
