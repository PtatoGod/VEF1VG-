Persona 5 Royal

What's it about?

Forced to transfer to a high school in Tokyo, the protagonist has a strange dream. “You truly are a prisoner of fate. In the near future, ruin awaits you.” With the goal of “rehabilitation” looming overhead, he must save others from distorted desires by donning the mask of a Phantom Thief.

Lead the Phantom Thieves of Hearts in this award winning, stylish turn-based RPG filled with dungeon crawling, party customization, strategic combat, and Persona fusion. Explore Tokyo, build relationships, battle Shadows, and infiltrate the minds of the corrupt to make them change their ways!

Source: Steam store page

Quick facts

  • Released: 2020 (2019 in Japan)
  • Platforms: PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch
  • Genre: Role-playing, social simulation
  • Length: About 100 hours (main story)
